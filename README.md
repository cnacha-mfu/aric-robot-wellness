# ARIC Robot Wellness — สร้างสมองให้หุ่นยนต์เพื่อการรักษาและดูแล

Concept deck for the Chair of the Mae Fah Luang University Council (October 2026): one story that runs from
why (ageing society, AI leaving the screen), to what ARIC builds (the brain for robots, first the brain for
treatment and care), how (the ARIC Spark–Forge–Launch engine and a small organisation), people first
(the Embodied Intelligence faculty-upskilling project), and the budget, five-year path and ask.

Live: https://cnacha-mfu.github.io/aric-robot-wellness/

Single self-contained `index.html` (1280×720 stage). Keys: ← → / Space to move, G for overview, R to replay
a slide's animation. Bump `<meta name="deck-version">` on every publish so open copies refresh themselves.

Shares its design, logo and the ARIC film with https://cnacha-mfu.github.io/aric-plan-deck/.
The ward photo on slide 5 is a frame from the AI-generated clips in the ARIC film.
